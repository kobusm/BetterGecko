BetterGecko 2.0

What's New
- Electricity cost tracking: set your electricity rate (R/kWh) and element rating (kW) in Settings. Defaults are R4.00/kWh and 4 kW.
- New Stats tiles show the estimated AC heating cost for this month and last month.
- The splash screen now shows the app version.
- Mode schedules: automatically switch operating mode at set times.
- Settings now load the live setpoints and mode from your Gecko.
- PV voltage is now shown at the correct scale.
- "Element Heating" is now labelled "AC Heating".

Note: monthly cost is estimated from AC heating time recorded while you use the app. Open the Stats tab regularly for the most accurate totals.
